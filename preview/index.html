Keratin & smoothening

Three to five months of smooth. Not three weeks of shine.

Most people who say “keratin ruined my hair” went to a studio that skipped two steps. Here is what actually happens at Glow Studio.

We start with a 15-minute strand assessment. If your hair has been bleached twice in the last three months, we say no to keratin and recommend hair botox or a repair plan first — we'd rather lose the bill than damage your hair.

Then: a clarifying wash, the protein treatment applied section by section (3–4 hours in the chair, so plan your day), a seal, and a finish. You leave with aftercare instructions written down, not just spoken, because what you use at home decides whether this lasts 3 months or 5.

Frequently asked

The questions we answer on WhatsApp every day.

How much does keratin cost in Visakhapatnam?
At Glow Studio it starts at ₹4,500 and usually lands between ₹4,500 and ₹8,000 depending on hair length and thickness. We confirm the exact price after the free 15-minute consultation, before we start. V
Will keratin damage my hair?
Done correctly, no. When the clarifying and sealing steps are rushed, hair comes out brittle. If your hair isn't ready for it, we'll say so and suggest hair botox or a repair plan instead.
Can I just walk in?
Yes, for haircuts, threading, waxing and facials. For keratin, smoothening, colour or bridal, message us first — those need 2–4 hours and we won't rush yours to fit someone else in.
Will the same person do my trial and my wedding-day makeup?
Yes. Sanjana handles bridal bookings herself, including the trial. Both your trial and your wedding date go into her calendar, not the studio's.
Do you charge extra once I'm sitting in the chair?
No. You get the final price in writing before we start, and it stays that price. Add-ons happen only if you ask for them.
How far in advance should I book a bridal trial?
For weddings between November and February, book 3–4 months ahead. Trials are limited to four per weekend since Sanjana does them personally, and November typically books out by mid-October.

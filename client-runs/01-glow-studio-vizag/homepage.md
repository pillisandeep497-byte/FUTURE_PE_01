## 10. FAQ

**"How much does keratin cost in Visakhapatnam?"**
At Glow Studio it starts at ₹4,500 and usually lands between ₹4,500 and ₹8,000 depending on hair length and thickness. We confirm the exact price after the 15-minute consultation, before we start. *(verify)*

**"Will keratin damage my hair?"**
Done correctly, no. It's when the clarifying and sealing steps are rushed that hair comes out brittle. If your hair isn't in a state to take it, we'll say so and suggest hair botox or a repair plan instead.

**"Can I just walk in?"**
Yes, for haircuts, threading, waxing and facials. For keratin, smoothening, colour or bridal, message us first — those need 2–4 hours and we won't rush yours to fit someone else in.

**"Will the same person do my trial and my wedding day makeup?"**
Yes. Sanjana handles bridal bookings herself, including the trial. Your trial and your wedding date both go in her calendar, not the studio's.

**"Do you charge extra once I'm sitting in the chair?"**
No. You get the final price in writing before we start, and it stays that price. Add-ons happen only if you ask for them.

> **Rationale:** Every question is written the way a customer actually asks on WhatsApp. Every answer gives a number, a process, or a policy — never "it depends, please contact us".

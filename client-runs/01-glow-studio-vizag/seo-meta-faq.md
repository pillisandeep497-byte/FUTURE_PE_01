## 6. FAQ — 8 publish-ready questions

**"What are the salon timings?"**
10 AM to 8:30 PM, all seven days, including Sundays and most public holidays. We close only for Ugadi and Sankranti. Last appointment starts at 7 PM for short services. *(verify)*

**"How much does a keratin treatment cost in Visakhapatnam?"**
At Glow Studio, keratin starts at ₹4,500 and usually falls between ₹4,500 and ₹8,000, depending on hair length and thickness. You get the exact price in writing after a free 15-minute consultation, before we start. *(verify)*

**"Do you have parking?"**
Two-wheelers can park directly outside the building. Cars park along MVP Colony Main Road — usually available except on Sunday evenings. The MVP Colony bus stop is a 4-minute walk.

**"Is threading available without an appointment?"**
Yes. Threading, waxing and haircuts are walk-in friendly on weekdays between 11 AM and 4 PM. On weekends and festival days, message us first — wait times can stretch to an hour.

**"Will colour or keratin damage my hair?"**
Not if it's done with the right assessment and products. We do a strand check before any chemical service, and a patch test 24 hours before colour. If your hair can't take a treatment, we'll tell you and suggest a repair plan instead.

**"Do you do bridal makeup outside Visakhapatnam?"**
Yes, for locations within about 60 km — Anakapalli, Vizianagaram, and nearby towns — with a travel charge that we confirm in advance. For farther locations, message us with the venue and date. *(verify radius)*

**"Can I get a price without coming to the studio?"**
Yes. Send a photo of your hair or skin on WhatsApp along with what you're hoping for. You'll get a price range and honest advice within 10 minutes during working hours. No appointment needed.

**"How far in advance should I book a bridal trial?"**
For weddings between November and February, book 3–4 months ahead. Trials are limited to four per weekend since Sanjana does them personally, and November trials typically fill by mid-October.

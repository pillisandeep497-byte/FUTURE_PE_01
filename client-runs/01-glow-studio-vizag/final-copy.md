## Frequently asked

**How much does keratin cost in Visakhapatnam?**
At Glow Studio it starts at ₹4,500 and usually lands between ₹4,500 and ₹8,000 depending on hair length and thickness. We confirm the exact price after the free 15-minute consultation, before we start. *(verify)*

**Will keratin damage my hair?**
Done correctly, no. When the clarifying and sealing steps are rushed, hair comes out brittle. If your hair isn't ready for it, we'll say so and suggest hair botox or a repair plan instead.

**Can I just walk in?**
Yes, for haircuts, threading, waxing and facials. For keratin, smoothening, colour or bridal, message us first — those need 2–4 hours and we won't rush yours to fit someone else in.

**Will the same person do my trial and my wedding-day makeup?**
Yes. Sanjana handles bridal bookings herself, including the trial. Both your trial and your wedding date go into her calendar, not the studio's.

**Do you charge extra once I'm sitting in the chair?**

## Services FAQ
**Will you tell me the price before I come in, or only after?**
Before. Send a photo and we'll give you a range on WhatsApp. The exact figure is confirmed in writing during the consultation, before we begin.

**Is a patch test compulsory for colour?**
Yes, for every colour service, at least 24 hours before. No exceptions, including for regular clients.

**Can I get keratin done in one day without a consultation first?**
We can do the strand assessment the same day if you have 3–4 hours free. If you're coming from far or you're on a deadline, come for the free consultation first — it's 15 minutes and it saves a wasted trip.

**Does smoothening make hair look flat and oily?**
It can, on the wrong hair type. That's why we ask about your hair history and check it before recommending smoothening over keratin.

**Do you charge more for long hair?**
Yes. Longer, thicker hair uses more product and more time. We confirm the difference before you book, not at the counter.

**What if I don't like the result?**
Tell us within 7 days and we'll fix it. If it was our error, the correction is free. If you want something different from what we agreed, we'll discuss the cost honestly before starting. *(verify policy)*

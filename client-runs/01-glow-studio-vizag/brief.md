- **Business name:** Glow Studio by Sanjana
- **Category:** Beauty salon & bridal studio
- **City / area:** Visakhapatnam (Vizag), Andhra Pradesh — MVP Colony Main Road, Sector 4, above Sri Sai Bakery
- **Landmarks:** 4 minutes' walk from MVP Colony bus stop; opposite the Sector 4 park entrance; 10 minutes from Sampath Vinayaka Temple
- **Years operating:** 7 (since 2019) *(verify)*
- **Team:** Sanjana (owner + senior stylist, 12 years in the trade), 2 senior stylists, 1 beautician, 1 assistant. 4 chairs.
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 8:30 PM, all days. Busiest: Sat & Sun 11 AM – 2 PM and 5 PM – 8 PM.

## 2. Customer
- **Primary customer:** Women aged 22–45 in Vizag — working professionals who want a low-maintenance, professional look, and brides-to-be planning a wedding in the next 3–12 months.
- **Secondary customer:** College students and teenage girls coming for a first haircut or first facial with a friend; working men for haircuts and beard styling (small share, do not lead with it).
- **What they want emotionally:** To stop being embarrassed by their hair. To be the one whose photos everyone compliments at the wedding. To walk into a studio and not feel judged or upsold.
- **What they fear:** Paying ₹6,000 and hating the result. Their hair becoming fried straw after keratin or colour. Being pressured into add-ons they didn't ask for. A trainee doing the work. The trial looking nothing like the wedding day.

## 4. Differentiation
- **Unique mechanism:** A **15-minute consultation before anything chemical happens**, and a **24-hour patch test before every colour service**. Sanjana's rule: *"If your hair can't take the treatment, we say no and give you a repair plan instead."* No other studio in the area advertises a patch test so openly.
- **Proof points:** 7 years in MVP Colony *(verify)*; 4,500+ clients served *(verify)*; 300+ brides done *(verify)*; uses **L'Oréal Professionnel** and **Schwarzkopf Professional** colour and **O3+** facials; senior stylists only — no trainee touches colour.
- **Process proof:** Every client gets a **written price before the service starts** — nothing is added to the bill mid-service.
- **Guarantee:** Free redo within 7 days if the colour or cut isn't what was agreed in the consultation. *(Confirm this is still policy.)*

## 7. Goals
- **Primary conversion action:** WhatsApp message (click-to-chat) — booked directly into the calendar.
- **Secondary conversion action:** Phone call during business hours; walk-in.
- **Seasonal urgency (real):** Wedding season runs November–February. Bridal trials are limited to **4 per weekend** and November usually fills by mid-October. Also: Saturday and Sunday slots for keratin/smoothening (3–4 hour services) are limited to 2 per day.
- **Response promise:** WhatsApp replies within 10 minutes during working hours.

## 8. Voice
- **Tone:** Friendly, warm, honest — with the confidence of someone who has done 300 weddings.
- **Language:** English, with light Telugu warmth allowed in WhatsApp/Instagram lines only. Hero stays English for search.
- **Must sound like:** honest, experienced, unhurried.
- **Must NOT sound like:** salesy, clinical, discount-driven, fake-luxury.
- **Words the owner hates:** "cheap", "discount parlour", "beauty parlour" (she calls it a studio), "pamper yourself".

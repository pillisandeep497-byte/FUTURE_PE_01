### 1. Primary booking CTA
**"Send us a photo of your hair and what you're hoping for. A stylist — not a bot — replies within 10 minutes between 10 AM and 8:30 PM, with a price range and an honest opinion on what's possible. No appointment needed to ask."** *(38 words)*

> **Rationale:** Response-time promise + micro-ask (photo, not booking) + the "not a bot" line, which is the exact doubt a local customer has about big-chain WhatsApp.

# Adaptability Proof — One Prompt System, Five Business Types

The task requires adaptable prompts that work for *multiple* local businesses. This file proves it: the identical structural prompt (PROMPT 01, with only the brief and tone profile swapped) generates five different voices.

**The proof:** same sections, same conversion skeleton — completely different rhythm, proof type and urgency in each. If your output, read side by side, could be pasted onto any of these sites unchanged, the system has failed. It cannot here.

## 2. Cafe / restaurant — FRIENDLY & WARM

**Business:** Filter & Froth Coffee House, Siripuram, Visakhapatnam
**H1:** *Filter coffee that takes 4 minutes. Not 4 minutes of your day wasted — 4 minutes that's the point.*
**Sub-headline:** *A 26-seat coffee house on Siripuram Road, roasting in small batches for people who'd rather sit with their coffee than carry it.*
**Value block (4 differentiators, on-pattern):**
- **Roasted 9 days ago, max** — every bag is date-stamped. If it's older, it's not on the shelf.
- **Chikmagalur single estate + a house blend** — we'll tell you which one's in your cup, and why.
- **26 seats, no laptop-loud policy before 4 PM** — the corner table is for conversations.
- **Chai is on the menu, unapologetically** — this is Vizag, not Melbourne.
**Proof type:** suppliers named, roast dates, seat count, regulars
**Urgency:** *The 8 AM first-brew rush; weekend seating after 5 PM*
**CTA:** `See what's brewing today` / `Walk in — we hold tables till 6:15 PM`
**What changed from the salon:** tone became sensory rather than outcome-based; proof moved from people to products and sourcing; urgency moved from season to daily rhythm.

## 3. Clinic / diagnostic centre — PROFESSIONAL tone

**Business:** Sri Sai Diagnostics & Polyclinic, Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam
**H1:** *Blood tests in the morning. Reports on your phone the same evening.*
**Sub-headline:** *A NABL-accredited lab in Gajuwaka with home sample collection across Visakhapatnam, and reports that reach you before your doctor's evening clinic.*
**Value block (4 differentiators, on-pattern):**
- **Same-day reports for 90% of routine tests** — samples are processed in-house, not couriered to another city.
- **Home collection in a 12 km radius, ₹150** *(verify)* — two phlebotomists, slots from 6:30 AM.
- **NABL-accredited, internal quality checks twice daily** — calibration logs available on request.
- **Results explained, not just printed** — a follow-up call for every abnormal value. *(verify process)*
**Proof type:** accreditation, equipment names, turnaround times, named doctors
**Urgency:** *Fasting-slot availability before 9 AM; health check-up packages during awareness months*
**CTA:** `Book a home collection` / `Download the test price list`
**What changed:** zero humour, precise units and timings, claims are anchored to accreditation and process. Urgency is scheduling-based, never fear-based. Tone profile used: PROFESSIONAL.

## 4. Coaching institute — CONFIDENT-SIMPLE tone

**Business:** Vidya Academy — IIT-JEE & EAMCET, MVP Colony, Visakhapatnam
**H1:** *EAMCET rank 5,000 is achievable in 11 months. Here is the exact weekly plan.*
**Sub-headline:** *Batches of 48 for Class 11 and 12 students in Vizag, taught by the same faculty who write the study material — with a test every Saturday and a parent update every month.*
**Value block (4 differentiators, on-pattern):**
- **48 students per batch. Not 120.** — every doubt gets answered in the same class, not after it.
- **Weekly test, weekly ranking, weekly doubt session** — you always know where you stand.
- **Faculty who've written the material they teach** — physics faculty, 14 years, 3 published workbooks. *(verify)*
- **Fee structure on one page, no hidden add-ons** — including the test-series cost and revision classes.
**Proof type:** results, rank bands, batch size, faculty tenure, a verifiable progression story
**Urgency:** *Batch starts 1st week of June; 2 seats left in the morning batch* (only if true)
**CTA:** `Attend 2 demo classes free` / `Download last year's results`
**What changed:** sentences got shorter and more declarative, numbers lead every line, emotional appeal shifted to confidence, and the proof unit is results rather than faces.

## 5. Freelancer / small agency — CONFIDENT-EXPERT tone

**Business:** Karthik R., freelance web developer (React/Next.js), Visakhapatnam
**H1:** *Website that loads in 1.4 seconds, hands-off after launch, no retainer trap.*
**Sub-headline:** *I build fast, editable websites for local businesses in Visakhapatnam and Hyderabad — fixed price, 3-week delivery, and you own every password on handover.*
**Value block (4 differentiators, on-pattern):**
- **Fixed quote after one call** — I don't bill hourly, so you can't get surprise invoices.
- **Load time target in writing** — under 1.8s on 4G, or I optimise free until it is. *(verify willingness)*
- **You own the code, the domain and the hosting** — no CMS lock-in, no "call us to change a phone number".
- **Two rounds of revisions, then ₹1,500 a round** — stated upfront, because scope creep kills small projects.
**Proof type:** case study numbers (before/after load times, conversion lift), named clients, an outcome metric
**Urgency:** *I take 2 projects a month; next slot opens mid-October*
**CTA:** `Send your site and get a quote in 24 hours`
**What changed:** the voice is first-person singular, proof is case-study numerical, and the CTA promises a quote rather than a visit. Tone profile: CONFIDENT-SIMPLE with expert detail.

## Side-by-side: what actually changes (the interview answer)

| Dimension | Salon | Cafe | Clinic | Coaching | Freelancer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 pattern | Outcome pair | Sensory claim | Speed proof | Achievable target + plan | Technical spec + freedom |
| Sentence rhythm | Short, warm | Short, sensory | Measured, precise | Punchy, numbers first | Direct, first-person |
| Proof unit | Faces, weddings | Sourcing, dates | Accreditation, turnaround | Ranks, faculty | Case-study metrics |
| Urgency mechanism | Season + capacity | Daily rush | Slot availability | Batch start + seats | Project slots |
| Emotional promise | Confident, unembarrassed | Belonging, ritual | Safety, certainty | Belief + a plan | Control, no lock-in |
| CTA verb | Send / ask | Walk in / see | Book / download | Attend / download | Send / get a quote |
| Never uses | Jargon | Corporate words | Slang, hype | Vague inspiration | Buzzwords |

**The constant across all five:** the 11-section skeleton (hero → problem → differentiators → services → proof → process → location → FAQ → CTA), one concrete detail per ~25 words, zero banned phrases, and every claim traceable. Structure is reusable; voice is not. That is the entire thesis of this prompt system.
